Kudisa is designed as ONE couples app, not a collection of separate apps.

Core areas:
- Home / Today
- Communication
- Music / Our Soundtrack
- Date Studio
- Couple Games
- Style Studio
- Memories
- Milestones
- Bucket List
- Wedding
- Farmhouse
- Life We Want
- Tasks / Chores
- Calendar / Important Dates / Reminders
- School Timetable
- Couple Profiles / Preferences
- 224 / BME relationship identity

Calendar requirements:
- recurring annual birthdays and anniversary
- custom events
- marked important dates
- countdowns
- configurable reminders
- in-app alerts
- browser notifications

2/2/2 after marriage:
- 2 weeks: evening out
- 2 months: weekend away
- 2 years: week away

Financial Life We Want:
- investment capital target = monthly income × 120
- minimum monthly investment = monthly income × 0.10
- emergency savings target = monthly income × 4
- monthly expenses = monthly income × 0.55

Production note:
The current static GitHub build provides client-side persistence and browser/in-app reminders. Reliable notifications while the app is closed should be connected to a push provider/backend and service worker in the production deployment.
